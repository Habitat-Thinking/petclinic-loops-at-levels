### Take the laugh away
TIME: 45s
SAY: This is the move the whole close depends on. Say it before the laugh settles.
SAY: Every one of those agents was told, explicitly, that finding nothing was a
SAY: perfectly good answer and that padding would corrupt the measurement.
SAY: Not one of them padded. And every single one found something true:

SAY: directive. A standard with no defined answer at all. And a question nobody
SAY: had asked - it is an upstream file.

### The turn
TIME: 45s
SAY: So the answer is not to build the habitat higher.
SAY: Level 5 is not where anyone should be trying to live. A habitat that can only
SAY: work at its highest setting is a badly tuned habitat.
SAY: What we have been building is not a ladder to the top. It is a repertoire.
SAY: And the skill is choosing the mode the work deserves.
SAY: Deliver it as a conclusion, not an apology. Four levels of evidence earned it.

TIME: 60s

SAY: fifty-two dollars.
SAY: The booking feature is Migrate: NOT NULL in three dialects, rows rewritten,
SAY: packages coupled. Reversal is a planned migration.
