### Five levels, and none of them lit yet
TIME: 45s
SAY: SLIDE 3. Never cuts. The talk's own visual language starts here.
SAY: The ladder, cold - the only time in the talk nothing is lit.
SAY: Name the five: Dictating, Commanding, Regulating, Orchestrating,
SAY: Supervising. Do not explain them; each has its own deck.
SAY: Say the branch fact once: every level is a git branch, and the diff between
SAY: two of them is a slide.
SAY: Then plant the caveat, and it is the ONLY forward reference the opener gets:
SAY: I am calling it a ladder because that is the order the evidence arrives in.
SAY: By the end I will be arguing it is not one.
SAY: That sentence stops the room assuming you are selling the top rung.
MODE: manual

TIME: 30s
